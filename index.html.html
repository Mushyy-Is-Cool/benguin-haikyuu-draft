Rally Draft reference

A complete, up-to-date guide to the modes, drafting systems, match simulator, bonuses, and player database.

At a glance

Rally Draft is a Haikyuu!!-inspired lineup builder. Every game uses the same seven volleyball roles, illustrated player avatars, role-weighted ratings, active connections, and a watchable three-set match.

Current database

Starter-screen game modes

Classic auction draftTwo teams receive $70 and fill a starting seven plus seven-player bench. Nominate a random player, bid, pass, and keep at least $1 for every open slot. Play locally or against Easy, Normal, or Hard bots.
Split-pair draftTwo local players draft seven starters only. Each round presents a random two-player pair for one position: the chooser claims one player and the other automatically joins the opponent. Players alternate the first six choices; a coin toss decides who chooses from the final, seventh pair. There is no bench or money.
Rally Cup pool draftBuild a seven-player team from randomly offered, shuffled position groups: choose 1 of 4 Setters, 2 of 8 Middle Blockers, 2 of 8 Outside Hitters, 1 of 4 Liberos, and 1 of 4 Opposite Hitters. Every new cup shuffles both the players and the order of those groups. Win a semi-final and final to take the cup.
Rally League seasonDraft seven starters from the same rotating position pools, then lead them through a five-week, six-club round robin. Play your fixture live each week while the other league results are simulated. The most league points after every club has played five matches wins the title; set difference breaks ties.
Bot personalities: Easy uses a balanced approach, Normal looks for synergy-building picks, and Hard prioritises elite attackers. All bots still obey the same lineup and budget limits.

Drafting and team rating

  • Classic roster: the starting seven are role-weighted; each bench player contributes 25% of their overall depth value.
  • No-bench modes: Split-pair and Rally Cup use the seven starters only. Their rating has no bench contribution.
  • Lineup management: after an auction, click compatible players on the same team to swap starters and bench before locking in.
  • Quick-draft: the Classic auction can complete the remaining bidding automatically when you want to move on.
  • Draft progress: the auction screen tracks partial and active duo/core connections for both teams so you can see what a pick may unlock.
  • Player abilities: every player has a short ability. Position-based abilities apply to all players, while notable characters have a named speciality. These influence relevant live actions such as setting, receiving, spiking, blocking, or serving.
Rating inputs: role fit, starter ratings, bench depth where allowed, active duo and school-core bonuses, and an active school passive all contribute to the displayed rating and win chance.

Live three-set match

Win chanceThe percentages above the court compare both starting lineups. Higher team quality improves the chance to win, while abilities, school passives, and game plans nudge rally outcomes.
Volleyball actionsEvery rally animates serve, receive, set, spike, and block actions. Middle Blockers are favoured for blocks, hitters for spikes, Setters for sets, and Liberos for receives.
Rotation and liberoTeams rotate on a side-out. A back-row Middle Blocker is replaced by the Libero, except when that middle is serving from position 1.
Watch controlsChoose 1×, 2×, or 4× speed; use a game plan; replay the same seeded match; or fast-forward to the final MVP result.
Sound and motionSound mix offers Full arena, Key moments, or Muted. It includes whistles, contact, hits, point cues, and celebrations. Reduce motion is available for a calmer display.
After the final whistleSee the set scores, a winner-only MVP, a compact highlight reel, and game stat tables for points, assists, spikes, sets, receives, and blocks.

Every synergy and school passive

A duo activates only when both named players are starters. A four-player school core requires any four starters from that school. The largest same-school starter group also activates its school passive at two or more starters, awarding +2 to +4 rating and strengthening a relevant rally action.

Active vs inactive: synergy panels explicitly show whether a connection is active, missing, or inactive because one or both partners are on the bench. In no-bench modes, every drafted player is automatically a starter.

Every player

Search by name or filter the complete roster. Ratings are on a 1–10 scale: ATH athleticism, STR strength, STA stamina, TEC technique, MEN mentality, OFF offence, DEF defence. Total is their combined stat score.

Each illustrated avatar uses a fixed, named reference profile for hair cut, colour, roots, highlights and facial details—never a generated silhouette—then wears their school kit with surname, jersey number and position on the back. The short ability beneath each name identifies the speciality used during live rallies.
